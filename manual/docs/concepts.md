# Concepts

The basic usage of this program is: when you want to start working, you make a clock in, and after finishing you make a clock out. For brevity, this is referred to as just a _clock_.

A clock needs to have a category. This is useful to distinguish between different clocks. So for example lets say you have two jobs: A, and B. You'll want to be able to distinguish clocks between these two jobs so you don't accidentally count hours that were worked on A which were really worked on B.

After making our clocks, you'll likely want to see a summary of them. This is known as a _report_. A report can show you a breakdown of the amount of hours you worked per day, and per week. You can filter to see reports for individual categories.
